01 / Automation + AI

Project Newzz

An end-to-end automated news platform built around a pipeline that takes raw articles and turns them into structured, readable news content with AI-generated imagery.

Python Flask Supabase Cloudflare AI News scraping
View live project →

The pipeline

01

Collect

News articles are scraped and brought into the pipeline as source material.

02

Understand

Articles are summarized and classified so the resulting content is easier to organize.

03

Generate

The title and summary are used to generate an article image through an AI image model.

04

Publish

Structured article data and generated imagery are stored and surfaced through the web application.

What I built

The interesting part of Newzz is not a single page. It is the connection between the pieces: collection, processing, storage, image generation, and the final reader-facing interface. The goal was to make that chain work as one system rather than as isolated demos.

Why it matters

It demonstrates practical automation and AI integration: taking an input that changes every day, processing it programmatically, persisting useful data, and turning that data into something people can browse.
